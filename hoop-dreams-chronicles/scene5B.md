## Scene 5B: The Power of Preparation

You decide that becoming a successful basketball player requires more than physical training.

Instead of practicing extra hours, you focus on resting, building confidence, and preparing mentally for your next big game.

You spend time watching professional basketball games, studying different strategies, and learning how great players make decisions under pressure.

Your coach encourages you to believe in yourself and trust your teammates.

Over the next few days, you feel stronger, more focused, and ready to compete.

On the day of an important practice game, your coach announces that several talented players will be competing for a starting position.

You know this is your chance to prove that preparation and confidence can lead to success.
