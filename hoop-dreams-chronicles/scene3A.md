## Scene 3A: The Winning Shot

The crowd is cheering as the final seconds of the neighborhood basketball tournament approach.

Your team is losing by two points, and the pressure is on you to make a difference.

You receive the ball from your teammate and decide to take the final three-point shot.

With only five seconds left, you dribble past a defender and prepare to shoot.

You jump high into the air and release the basketball toward the hoop.

The crowd goes silent as everyone watches the ball fly through the air.

Suddenly, the ball goes through the net!

The crowd erupts in celebration. Your teammates rush toward you, cheering your name.

Your incredible performance catches the attention of a basketball scout watching from the sidelines.

The scout approaches you and offers you an opportunity to attend a special basketball training camp.
