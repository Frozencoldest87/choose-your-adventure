# Hoop Dreams – Chronicles 🏀

## Scene 2A: The First Tryout

You decide to join your school basketball team because you believe this is your first step toward achieving your NBA dream.

The next morning, you arrive at the school gym. The sound of bouncing basketballs and squeaking sneakers fills the air.

Dozens of talented players are competing for a spot on the team.

Coach Williams blows his whistle and announces:

"Today, I want to see your skills, teamwork, and determination. Only the best players will make this team!"

As the tryouts begin, you notice two opportunities to impress the coach.

You could demonstrate your incredible shooting skills, or you could focus on passing and helping your teammates score.

You know this decision could determine whether you make the team.
