# Hoop Dreams – Chronicles 🏀

## Scene 7B: The Power of Unity

With only a few minutes remaining in the basketball game, your team is struggling against a strong opponent.

Instead of trying to win the game alone, you decide to trust your teammates and work together.

You encourage everyone to communicate, pass the ball, and support one another.

Your team begins playing with confidence and determination.

As the final seconds approach, your teammate makes an incredible shot that brings the game to a tie.

The game goes into overtime, and your team works together to secure an exciting victory.

After the game, your coach congratulates everyone for showing great teamwork and leadership.

The next afternoon, you return to the basketball courts to practice your jump shots.

While practicing, several friends approach you and mention two exciting competitions taking place nearby.

One friend invites you to participate in a dunk contest, while another suggests joining a three-point shooting contest.

You must decide which opportunity to pursue.
