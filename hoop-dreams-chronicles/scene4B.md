# Hoop Dreams – Chronicles 🏀

## Scene 4B: Staying Loyal to Your Team

After winning the neighborhood basketball tournament, you receive an exciting offer to join a professional basketball training program.

However, you decide to stay with your neighborhood team because you believe in loyalty, friendship, and teamwork.

Your teammates are excited about your decision. They promise to work harder together to achieve bigger dreams.

Over the next few weeks, your team practices every evening at the local basketball court.

One afternoon, your coach announces that a major city basketball tournament will take place soon.

The winning team will have an opportunity to compete against some of the best young basketball players in the region.

You realize this could be another chance to prove your talent and move closer to your NBA dream.

However, your team must decide how to prepare for the tournament.
