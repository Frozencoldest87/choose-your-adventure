## Scene 7A: The Comeback

With only a few minutes remaining in the basketball game, your team is losing by four points.

You decide to take responsibility and lead your teammates in an incredible comeback.

You encourage everyone to stay focused and remind them that the game is not over until the final whistle.

Your team begins playing with determination. You score an important three-pointer, and your teammate makes a successful layup.

The crowd erupts in celebration as your team takes the lead.

After the game, your coach congratulates you for your leadership and determination.

The following afternoon, you return to the basketball courts to practice your jump shots.

While practicing, you notice several friends gathering near two exciting basketball competitions.

One friend invites you to participate in a dunk contest, while another mentions a three-point shooting competition.

You realize that another important decision awaits you.
