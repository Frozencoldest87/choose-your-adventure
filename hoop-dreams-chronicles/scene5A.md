# Hoop Dreams – Chronicles 🏀

## Scene 5A: The Extra Practice

You decide to dedicate extra hours to improving your basketball skills.

Whether preparing for the academy scouts or helping your neighborhood team, you know that hard work can make a difference.

Every morning, you wake up early to practice shooting, dribbling, passing, and defense.

Your teammates notice your dedication and begin joining your extra training sessions.

After several weeks, your skills improve, and your confidence grows.

However, the additional training leaves you exhausted.

One afternoon, your coach notices that you are struggling to keep up during practice.

He reminds you that becoming a great basketball player requires both hard work and proper recovery.
