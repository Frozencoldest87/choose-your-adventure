# Hoop Dreams – Chronicles 🏀

## Scene 3B: The Power of Teamwork

The final seconds of the neighborhood basketball tournament are ticking away.

Your team is losing by two points, and everyone expects you to take the final shot.

Instead, you notice your teammate standing wide open near the three-point line.

You quickly pass the basketball to your teammate.

With only three seconds remaining, your teammate takes the shot.

The ball flies through the air and drops perfectly through the hoop!

The crowd erupts in celebration as your team wins the championship.

Your teammates surround you, thanking you for trusting them.

A basketball coach watching the tournament is impressed by your leadership and teamwork.

After the game, the coach approaches you with an exciting opportunity to join a basketball development program.
