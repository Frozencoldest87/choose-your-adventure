# Hoop Dreams – Chronicles 🏀

## Scene 4A: The Training Camp

After your impressive performance at the neighborhood basketball tournament, you accept an invitation to attend a special basketball training camp.

At the camp, you meet talented basketball players from different schools and neighborhoods. Everyone dreams of becoming a professional athlete.

The training sessions are challenging. You practice shooting, passing, defense, and teamwork every day.

One afternoon, the head coach announces that scouts from a major basketball academy will attend the final practice game.

You realize this could be your biggest opportunity to achieve your dream of playing in the NBA.

However, you must decide how to prepare for the important game.
